# Poda fork assurance route

## State

hypothesis

## Promise

Every Poda Web product change can be judged on its exact pull-request head by a
fork-owned CI route that runs the declared static, behavioral, branding,
localization, accessibility, and production-network checks and retains
retrievable evidence for every claimed result.

## Scope

The promise covers the Poda-specific web-client gate, its approved CI trigger
and runner route, produced artifacts, and Bedrock Witness records for Poda Web
pull requests into `internal/main`.
