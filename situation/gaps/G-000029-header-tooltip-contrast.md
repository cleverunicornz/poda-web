# Room header tooltips are hard to read in Poda Light

## State

open

## Gap

Room header tooltips, including the Poda Profile widget button's, render dark
text on a near-black tooltip in Poda Light. Which theme token causes it is
unknown.

## Relevance

[P-000021](situation/promises/P-000021-poda-profile-side-panel.md) P5 header
button; [G-000024](situation/gaps/G-000024-placeholder-and-accent-contrast.md)
records other contrast concerns.

## Evidence

In the [W-000006](situation/witnesses/P-000021/W-000006-profile-side-panel-local-pass.md)
run, `header-button-widget-in-side-panel.png` shows the "Poda Profile" tooltip
as a near-black box with barely visible text; the "People" tooltip on the member
facepile showed the same.

## Impact

Users hovering header buttons may not learn what they do.

## Resolution

none
