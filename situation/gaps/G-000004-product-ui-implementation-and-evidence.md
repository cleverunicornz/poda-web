# Product UI implementation and evidence remain absent

## State

open

## Gap

The newly selected frontend product guardrails do not supply an implemented product slice, a qualified service contract, executable product Oracles or retained product Witnesses. P-000009 through P-000014 are hypotheses and their matching Oracles are designed/manual; the available Element extension and preview source establishes candidate seams, not product feasibility or assurance.

## Relevance

The selected rendering, native-assistance, artifact, host-extension and preview boundaries constrain later separately approved work. They must not be mistaken for a delivered manual/assisted artifact product, public Astro integration or a new implementation assignment.
